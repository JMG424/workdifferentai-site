Somewhere along the way

We learned to look outside of ourselves for the answers.

We compare. We overthink. We people please. We perform. We overgive. We make ourselves smaller.

Self-love is not pretending you’re fine. It is telling yourself the truth, and staying kind while you do it.

That is what this class is. One evening, one story, and one practice you can use the next time something sets you off.

In the class

What you’ll experience

  1. Her real story

    From her hardest years to her turning point. The self-hatred, the wins that didn’t fix it, and what finally did.

  2. One simple method

    Connect, Know, Trust, Choose. Clear enough to remember and use right away.

  3. A live practice

    Bring a recent trigger. Look at your usual reaction. Pause. Find a better choice.

  4. A meditation and a love letter

    A guided meditation, then time to write a love letter to yourself, so you leave with something real.

No slides. Kristiana teaches from her story, and you do the work with her.

The tool you’ll practice live

The sacred pause

  1. Notice the feeling.
  2. Take the pause.
  3. Choose the action that fits who you really are.

Your feelings are information. They are not who you are.

Who this is for

This is for people who are ready to look.

  • You’ve done the things that were supposed to make you feel like enough, and you still don’t.
  • You know your patterns. You’d like to stop repeating them.
  • You want something you can practice, not another thing to read.

If that’s not you right now, that’s fine. If it is, come.

Your teacher

Kristiana Morell

She teaches from her own story: years of feeling disconnected from herself, looking outside for validation, and being afraid to trust the voice telling her to take a different path.

“I’m someone who is still doing this work right alongside you.”
