What I do

Concrete work. Written down. Checkable.

Engagements stay close to the operation. If a system cannot be evaluated, handed off, or run when you are not in the room, it is not finished.

  1. 01

    AI agent systems

    Design and stand up single- and multi-agent workflows for a defined job: tools, handoffs, failure modes, and who owns the outcome when the model is wrong.

  2. 02

    Evaluation and QA

    Put gates around model output before it reaches a customer, a ledger, or a file of record. Rubrics, sampling, and review loops you can actually run.

  3. 03

    Ops and automation

    Map the real operation — inbox, research, follow-up, scheduling — and automate the parts that should never have needed someone watching a screen.

  4. 04

    Written clarity

    Specs, SOPs, and runbooks so the system is explainable. If it cannot be written down, it cannot be delegated or checked.

  5. 05

    Remote agent ops

    Stay in the loop after install: cadence reviews, failure triage, and tightening the stack as the work changes.

How it works

Three steps. No theater.

  1. 01

    Name the work

    A short conversation. What has to happen, what must not fail, and what “good” looks like in your operation.

  2. 02

    Design the system

    Agents, tools, SOPs, and QA gates — written first, then built. Scope stays small enough to ship.

  3. 03

    Run the first cycles

    Watch real output together, fix what breaks, and leave you with a loop you can run without me in the chair.
