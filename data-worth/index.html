    How this works

    Four steps. You keep control at each one.

    1. Ballpark. The number above. If it isn’t interesting, stop here. No harm done.
    2. A conversation, under NDA. Thirty minutes. I ask questions about the data, the rights, and the history. You decide whether it’s worth going further.
    3. Export and package. You export from Salesforce, Xero and the industry system. We strip what shouldn’t travel, document the rights, and shape it into what a lab can actually use.
    4. Labs buy. You get paid. I take it to the labs. Success-based: nothing changes hands until something sells.

    Half of what makes data valuable is what people did with it.

    The steps they took. The judgment calls. The checks they ran before signing off. That record already lives in the tools your team uses every day.
